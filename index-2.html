It started with a microphone and a simple question

Where Are You Going began as a series of unscripted street interviews — no agenda, just a question and a willingness to listen. What we heard again and again was that people already knew what they needed. They just didn't have a way to get there.

So the foundation grew out of the interviews themselves: every conversation feeds into how we direct support, who we connect people with, and what "help" actually looks like in practice.

Who we are

Where Are You Going is a community foundation based in Columbus, Ohio, founded in 2025. We're currently operating independently while we work toward formal nonprofit registration — we'll update this page with our official status as soon as that's complete. Our street interviews and support programs currently focus on the Columbus area. Information submitted through this site — including help requests — is used only to connect you with support and is never sold or shared beyond that purpose. See our Privacy Policy for full details.
