Where Are You Going ("we," "us," "the foundation") respects the privacy of everyone who visits this site, submits a help request, volunteers, donates, or shares an answer to our question. This policy explains what information we collect, why, and how it's handled.

What we collect

  • Help requests: your name, a way to contact you (phone or email), the category of need you select, and any details you choose to share.
  • Volunteer sign-ups: your name, email, area of interest, and any notes you add.
  • Shared answers: the text you submit to "So… where are you going?" and whether you've given permission for it to appear on the homepage.
  • Donations: payment is processed entirely by Stripe. We do not receive or store your card details — Stripe's own privacy policy governs that information.
